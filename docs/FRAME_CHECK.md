# FRAME CHECK (law, Xavier 2026-11-19 08:04 + 09:21). Nothing reaches Xavier until EVERY frame passed this.
How: extract every frame, tile them (6x4, nothing skipped), then zoom crops of every face / hand / contact. A thumbnail
glance is not a check. Fix, re-render, re-check until a full pass finds nothing.

## 1. Research applied in every frame (research/cinematography.md, animation.md, lipsync.md, docs/ANIMATION.md)
- Composition: one clear subject; eyes on the upper third; look room toward who they face; foreground / subject /
  background layers; the speaker's face READS (not turned away, not blocked, not covered by hair or a foreground head).
- Lens + depth: long lens (fov 25-40), focus on the subject's eyes, foreground and background fall off.
- Light: designed key on the subject's face, rim separating them from the background, fill low; dark front, mid subject,
  bright back. No flat even light, no black faces.
- Posing: silhouette reads, line of action, power centre, weight / contrapposto, no twinning, head with the body,
  shoulder-vs-elbow limbs, hands ON what they touch.
- Face acting: expression matches the beat (brows lead), eyes look where the character looks, blinks motivated.
- Mouths: flap follows the audio (open on syllables, shut in pauses and on M B P), shut when not speaking.
- Grade: the look B grade, no vignette, no bloom wash.
## 2. Defects
Clipping, floating / detached limbs, skin showing through eyes, white rims / jagged edges on faces, wrong-way props,
props not where the hand is, flicker (a state on screen for 1 frame), pops between frames, blocked or unreadable shots,
anything a viewer would screenshot to laugh at.
