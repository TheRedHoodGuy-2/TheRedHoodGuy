# WORLD PLAN (Xavier 2026-11-18): two designed houses + one reusable town

Rule: design first, assets second. Every room starts from real modern-interior references, gets ONE design (palette,
materials, furniture family, lighting), and every piece in it is pulled or modelled to match, then re-coloured with our
materials so nothing clashes. Each step ends with a picture for Xavier.

## Step 1. Design boards (approval before any building)
- Research real modern homes: floor plans for a 2-storey family house (US suburban, 3-4 bedrooms), and interior design
  references for every room. Cited, in research/house_design.md.
- House A (guy's family): floor plan (both floors, basement, attic) + per-room design card (palette swatches, floor,
  walls, furniture list, light).
- House B (girl's family): a different plan and a different style + her room.
- Picture: one board per house.

## Step 2. Furniture library per design
- For each design, the full furniture list sourced (Creator Store pulls re-skinned, Poly Haven, or modelled properly),
  recoloured to the board's palette. Sheet per house for approval.

## Step 3. Build the rooms (one room at a time, a still each)
House A: living room, kitchen, dining, downstairs toilet, hall + stairs, parents' bedroom + bathroom, his bedroom,
shower/bathroom, storage/laundry, basement, attic. Then House B the same.

## Step 4. House shells (outside)
Exteriors that match the floor plans (so a window seen from inside is the same window outside), porch, yard, drive.

## Step 5. The town
- Base it on a real, good-looking neighbourhood (US suburb): street layout, lot sizes, house spacing.
- Map: residential streets with varied houses (shells), main street (shops, diner, supermarket), school, police station,
  fire station, hospital, park, library, church, gas station. All outer shells, insides only when a story needs one.
- Built for speed: detail only where the camera is (LOD), instanced trees/grass, the same lighting rules.
- Picture: an overview map + a few street-level stills.
