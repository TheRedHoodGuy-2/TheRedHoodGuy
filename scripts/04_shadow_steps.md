Title: Every lie moves your shadow 👣
(full script and checks: see the chat reply of 2026-11-19 10:xx; the text below is the script)

In this town, every lie makes your shadow step away from you.
You see, ten lies, ten steps, and your shadow leaves for good.
And once it's gone, nobody can see you anymore.
So liars here are easy to spot.
My teacher's shadow sits two desks behind her.
Our mailman says "Nice dog!" every single day.
His shadow lives at the end of the street.
And Grandpa once told Grandma her meatloaf was amazing.
We still set him a plate.
Just in case.
But lying isn't my problem.
My problem is my sister, Mia.
Mia lies all day.
To Mom. To teachers. To the dog.
And her shadow never moves.
Not one step.
So Mom thinks Mia is perfect.
Me? I never take off my red hoodie.
Mia never takes out her ponytail.
Until Tuesday.
Grandma's vase hit the floor.
Mia pointed right at me.
"He did it," she said.
And my shadow took a step away.
"I didn't!" I yelled.
But Mom didn't even look at me.
"He ate the last cookie too," Mia said.
Step.
"He said you look old, Mom."
Step.
Seven steps. Eight.
My hands were going see-through.
I looked down at my shadow.
It had a ponytail.
Then I looked at Mia's shadow.
It had a hood.
That was never her shadow.
It was mine.
Every lie Mia ever told was walking MY shadow out the door.
Nine steps.
I grabbed Mom's arm.
She looked right through me.
Mia smiled, and leaned in close.
"Mom," she whispered, "I love you."
