# 05 Sunset Tag (draft v1, 2026-11-19) — title: "In This Town, Tag Swaps You 🌅"
Narrator: the boy (red hoodie). Homeboy: Jayden. Shown-not-said cliffhanger at the end.

1. In this town, if you get tagged at sunset, you swap.
2. You see, boys turn into girls. Girls turn into boys.
3. It lasts until the next sunset.
4. So at 5:59, grown men sprint home from work.
5. The mailman delivers on rollerblades.
6. And my uncle got tagged once.
7. For one whole day, he was Aunt Linda.
8. We don't talk about Aunt Linda.
9. But I had a problem. His name was Jayden.
10. My homeboy. Fastest kid on the block.
11. He had never been tagged. Not once.
12. And every summer, he tagged me.
13. I've been a girl named Kayla three times.
14. So this summer, I practiced one thing.
15. Falling. Over and over.
16. Then Friday came. The whole block played.
17. The sun touched the hills. Ten seconds.
18. Jayden was right behind me.
19. Five seconds.
20. So I fell.
21. Jayden stopped. "Bro, you good?"
22. He reached down to help me up.
23. Two seconds.
24. I grabbed his hand. "Tag," I whispered.
25. The sun went down.
26. Jayden looked at his hands. Then at his hair.
27. "Bro," she said. "What did you DO?"
