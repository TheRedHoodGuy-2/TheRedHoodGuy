# 07 RESTART DAY ("In This Town, Mistakes Restart Your Day 🔁") draft v1 2026-11-25
Concept borrowed from Re:Zero (looping back after a failure); nothing else from it. Boy narrator, in-this-town format.
Visual rule prop: a number on everyone's wrist = how many times they have restarted.

## Script
In this town, if you mess up, your whole day restarts. Only you remember.
You see, the number on your wrist counts your tries.
My neighbor never fails a test. His wrist says 312.
The pizza guy never gets lost anymore. 1,050.
The guy asking out the lifeguard is on 4,000.
Mine always said one.
Mom says one is the best number. It means you got your day right.
She always wore a big watch over hers. She never took it off.
But I messed up a Tuesday.
At six p.m., Mom drove out to get my birthday cake.
She never came home.
Then I woke up. Seven a.m. Tuesday. Again.
My wrist said two.
So I hid her car keys.
She took the bus. Three.
I faked being sick.
She went at lunch instead. Nine.
I begged. I cried. I locked every door.
Forty-seven.
Every Tuesday, at six, the phone rang.
And every Tuesday, she didn't come home.
Then I learned the worst part.
At one hundred, the day stops restarting.
You keep whatever day you get.
My wrist said ninety-nine.
That's when I looked at the empty cake box.
She only ever left for one thing.
So on try ninety-nine, I made the cake myself.
Flour on the ceiling. Eggshells in the batter.
Six o'clock. She was still home.
Six thirty. She was laughing at my lopsided cake.
Seven. The phone never rang.
I hugged her so hard my arms hurt.
And for the first time in ninety-nine days, I woke up on Wednesday.
[SHOWN, NO VOICE ~4 s] Morning. Mom at the sink, sleeves pushed up, her big watch lying on the counter. The camera
slides down her arm to her wrist: 4,812. She catches him looking, smiles, and puts a finger to her lips. CUT.
