# Shorts strategy research (2026-11-15, Xavier: "we need a new strat")

Sources:
- Zack D Films "How Stitches Work" (253M views) breakdown, danielrusnok.substack.com/p/how-a-faceless-channel-engineered:
  question stated in second 1, ONE narrow question per video, ends the moment it is answered, 31 s,
  title = few words + one emoji, empty description.
- metricool.com/youtube-trends: per-Short performance best at 2-4 uploads a week, no ceiling on monthly total.
- quso.ai / ytgrowth.io / easyviral.ai: ~70% viewed (vs swiped) is the gate to wider distribution; under 50% the test pool dies.

Our data (see channel_stats_2026-11-15.md): tickle 69.6% / hypnic 68.1% stayed vs yawn 43.8, comeback 48.5, halloween 42.7.
Pattern: winners = a physical thing YOUR BODY does that you can't control. Losers = social/emotional or prank topics.

Strategy proposed to Xavier:
1. Topic filter: body + stakes / mild gross-scary curiosity ("what happens if...", "why you shouldn't...").
2. Payoff shows the REAL mechanism inside the body (zoom into him), not only the Brain HQ office metaphor.
3. Frame 0 title + action (rule since 2026-11-15).
4. Volume 3-4 a week: final at 1080x1920 (render ~2x faster), reuse sets/props.
