# Look study: Ink Game (GibsonTV Roblox film), 2026-10-28

Full notes: `reference/inkgame/NOTES.md` (360 cuts in 705 s, median shot 1.4 s).
Short version of what makes it not flat:
1. Lens: shallow depth of field in almost every shot, low camera heights, over-the-shoulder with a blurred shoulder,
   motion blur on fast moves, shake on impacts.
2. Light: every space has a named key light (tube, window, lamp, sun), real falloff, dark soft shadows, rim light, its own
   colour world per location, haze / dust in the air, a filmic grade with grain.
3. Materials: R6 blocks stay simple but every surface reads as what it is; wear only at contact points (edges, walk
   paths, under things). Clean in the middle, dirty where touched.
What Xavier kept from it: look B grade (DoF, contrast, grain, slight fringe) with NO vignette and NO bloom; NO cloth folds,
NO impact freezes (2026-10-28).
