# What real surfaces look like (researched 2026-10-28)

## US suburban house exteriors
- Siding colours people actually use: white, cream, butter yellow, beige / tan, light blue, blue-grey, deep blue, sage,
  barn red. Strong green is not a thing.
- Nearly always white trim, a darker roof, an accent colour on the front door.
- Walls are horizontal lap siding (clapboard) with a shadow line under each board; roofs are asphalt shingles in rows;
  columns / rails / fences white painted wood; decks and steps honey varnished wood; paths concrete, roads asphalt.
- Sources: [Family Handyman: popular siding colors](https://www.familyhandyman.com/list/popular-home-siding-colors/),
  [Allura USA: exterior colour combinations](https://www.allurausa.com/blog/color-combination-home-exterior),
  [Angi: popular siding colors](https://angi.com/articles/popular-siding-colors.htm)

## Home interiors / kitchens
- Warm white painted walls (light plaster feel), white painted doors, trims, skirting and ceilings.
- Wood floors (varnished planks), wood furniture in walnut or honey oak (never orange), white ceramic plates.
- Kitchens: white or wood cabinets, white quartz counters.
- Source: [Houzz: most popular new kitchens 2026](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/the-10-most-popular-new-kitchens-so-far-in-2026-stsetivw-vs~184899878)

## Classrooms
- Floors: vinyl composition tile (VCT), waxed, speckled ~30 cm squares with matte seams. Walls: painted concrete block.
- Desks: wood-look tops on metal legs; lockers painted steel; drop ceiling tiles.
- Source: [Spaces4Learning: walls, ceilings and floors](https://spaces4learning.com/articles/2020/12/01/walls-ceilings-floors.aspx)
