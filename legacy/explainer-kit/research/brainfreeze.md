# Brain freeze (2026-11-14)
- Trigger: something very cold touches the roof of the mouth (palate) / back of the throat. Blood vessels there constrict, then
  rapidly dilate as warm blood rushes in. Nerves wrapped round those vessels (trigeminal nerve) fire, and the pain is felt as
  "referred pain" in the forehead / temples. Medical name: sphenopalatine ganglioneuralgia.
- Fix: stop the cold, warm the palate: press the tongue (or the underside of a curled tongue) against the roof of the mouth,
  or sip warm water. Also: hold cold food at the front of the mouth a few seconds before swallowing.
