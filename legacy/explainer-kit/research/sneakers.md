# Sneakers: how a real one is built (for the "JADANS" knockoff in topics/comeback, 2026-11-13)

Why: Xavier (2026-11-12): models must look like the real thing, research it first; the roast gag needs obvious fake-brand
sneakers ("Jadans" instead of Jordans), built like a real high-top, not blocks.

Parts, top to bottom (what we model):
- Upper: the whole top part. Toe box at the very front where the toes sit; the TOE CAP / mudguard is an overlay piece on
  the front for protection and structure (often a different colour). The VAMP runs from the toe box back to where the
  laces start. The QUARTER is the side panel; the EYESTAY is the reinforced strip with the lace holes; the TONGUE sits
  under the laces and pokes up out of the collar.
- Collar: the padded opening around the ankle; high-cut on Air Jordan 1 style shoes, mid or low on others.
- Heel counter: stiff piece at the back that holds the heel, usually covered by a heel overlay panel.
- Midsole: the thick (usually white) cushioning layer under the upper; Outsole: the rubber bottom (gum / colour break),
  with a little TOE SPRING (the toe tip curves up off the ground).
- Details that sell it: contrast stitch lines, a side logo (die-cut overlay), a tongue label, a pull tab on the heel.

Classic basketball high-top colourway to riff on: white base, red overlays (toe cap, eyestay, heel, collar), black collar,
white midsole, red outsole. Our knockoff uses its OWN made-up logo (a lopsided wing-star) + the misspelt "JADANS" wordmark,
no real brand marks.
