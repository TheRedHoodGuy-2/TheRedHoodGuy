# Swallowing gum (2026-11-15)
- Gum base (elastomers, resins, waxes) can't be digested; sugar and flavour are. Britannica, Mental Floss, ScienceABC.
- It is pushed along by peristalsis and passes intact in the stool, usually in 1-3 days (Healthline ~40 h). The 7-year claim is a myth.
- Only large amounts swallowed in a short time (often with other indigestible stuff) can block the intestine (Healthline, Cleveland Clinic).
Sources: britannica.com/story/what-happens-when-you-swallow-gum, healthline.com/health/swallowed-gum, scienceabc.com/humans/what-happens-when-you-accidentally-swallow-a-chewing-gum
