# Props for the time-crawl video (2026-11-14)

## PS5 DualSense controller (fake brand on screen: no PlayStation logo)
- Real size about 160 mm wide x 66 mm thick x 106 mm deep, 280 g. Chunkier than the PS4 DualShock 4.
- Two-tone "Stormtrooper" look: white outer shell over a black core. Light bar runs around both sides of the touchpad
  (no rear light bar like the DS4). Big flat touchpad top centre (clickable). Create button left of the touchpad, Options
  right, PS button + mic mute below it between the sticks.
- Layout: D-pad top left, face buttons (triangle / circle / cross / square, now plain grey-white symbols) top right, two
  sticks low and symmetric in the black centre. L1/R1 bumpers + bigger curved L2/R2 triggers on top. Two grips (horns)
  angle down and out. USB-C on the top edge, headset jack + speaker grille at the bottom.
- Sizing rule (Xavier): sized to the R6 hands, not cm. The grips sit in both hand blocks, the body spans between them.
- Sources: fifplay.com/playstation-5-controller-guide, psu.com DualSense specs, gamespot.com DualSense design article,
  techcrunch.com 2020-04-07 DualSense reveal, techradar.com DualSense light bar.

## Hand tally counter (Brain HQ second counter)
- Classic hand tally (Veeder-Root style): round chrome / metal case about 50 x 55 mm, 4-digit window (white digits on
  black wheels), thumb push button on top, a finger ring underneath to hold it, a small reset knob on the side.
- Each thumb press adds one. Sources: Veeder-Root hand tally datasheet (novapart.co farnell 767086), Line Seiki H-102
  (directindustry.com).

## Wristwatch
- Kid's everyday watch: black resin digital sports watch (Casio-style): square-ish case with 4 side buttons, grey LCD
  with big digits, strap with holes + buckle. Worn on the left wrist.

## The game on the TV: Blood Strike style
- Blood Strike (NetEase, 2023): free FPS / battle royale, playable "Strikers" with abilities (drones, shield walls),
  cross-play, fast gunfights. Sources: store.steampowered.com/app/3199170, gog.com dreamlist Blood Strike,
  games.gg/blood-strike.
- On screen we show a FAKE-brand knockoff ("BLOOD STRIKER" style name, no real logo): first-person view, gun in the
  bottom right, crosshair, minimap top left, health + armour bars bottom left, ammo count bottom right, kill feed top
  right, enemies on a sunny urban map, muzzle flashes, hit markers.
