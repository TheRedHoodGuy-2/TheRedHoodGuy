# Lucky Me v1 (voice script, 2026-11-27). Title: Bad luck makes you RICH here 💸
In this town, the unluckier you are, the richer you get.
You see, every bad thing pays.
My neighbor got hit by lightning three times. Now he owns a mansion.
The richest man in town? His jet crashed into his own pool. Fifty million.
But I've never been unlucky. Not once.
My best friend Jaden is the unluckiest kid alive. And he's always right next to me.
Then Mom sat me down.
MOM: "If we don't pay rent by Friday, we lose the house."
I needed one bad thing to happen to me. Just one.
Monday, I stood outside in a thunderstorm.
The rain stopped. Right over me. A rainbow came out.
Tuesday, I kicked the vending machine that eats everyone's money.
Five free sodas fell out.
Wednesday, I bought a lottery ticket. I wanted to lose.
I won the flipping jackpot. But kids can't freaking cash it.
Friday. Four fifty-nine. The landlord knocked.
Then Jaden pulled up in a brand-new sports car.
JADEN: "Need some money, bro?"
