# Match Day v3 (sus pass, 2026-11-26)
Title: Every kid looks like their dad... except me 😳
COLD OPEN: Match Day, Dad beside me, teacher squinting, the mailman's van parked outside the classroom window.
In this town, every kid looks exactly like their dad.  [Jake and his dad, same mustache, waving in sync]
Jake has a mustache. He's seven.
The Miller triplets are three tiny Mr. Millers.  [three bald kids in ties]
But I look nothing like my dad.  [Dad afro, me spiky]
Mom says I'll grow into it.  [into her coffee]
Mom orders a package every single day. She always answers the door herself.  [boxes stacked to the ceiling, Mom fixing her hair before opening]
Dad works nights.  [Dad leaving with a lunchbox as the sun sets]
And the mailman always waves at me. Only me.  [cap low, wave]
Then my teacher said, "Friday is Match Day. Stand next to your dad. If you don't match, you sit in the corner."
I had three days.  [3 DAYS]
Day one, I drew Dad's mustache on myself. With permanent marker.
Day two, I wore his glasses. I walked into the fridge.
Day three, I asked Mom why I don't look like Dad. She dropped a whole plate.  [crash, Mom frozen]
Friday. Dad stood next to me. Permanent mustache and all.  [FRIDAY 9:00]
My teacher squinted at us for a long time.
Then the door opened. "Package for room four!"  [mailman walks in]
[no voice: every kid turns from the mailman to me. Same face.]
Teacher: "Oh! He matches!"
[no voice: the camera pans across the class: two more kids slowly turn around. Same face. The mailman waves. Cut on Dad.]
