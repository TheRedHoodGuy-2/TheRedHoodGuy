# 07 DO-OVERS ("In This Town, You Get 100 Do-Overs ⏪") draft v2 2026-11-25
Concept borrowed from Re:Zero (rewinding after a failure); nothing else from it. Boy narrator, in-this-town format.
Rule prop: a number on everyone's wrist that COUNTS DOWN from 100. At zero you never wake up.
Twist (shown, never said): his number never drops because Mom was paying for every rewind. Her wrist ends at 1.

## Script
In this town, everyone gets a hundred do-overs. Mess up, and your day rewinds.
You see, the number on your wrist goes down every time you use one.
My neighbor wasted forty on math tests.
The pizza guy spent ninety getting lost.
The guy asking out the lifeguard has three left.
And when you hit zero, you don't wake up.
Mine still said a hundred. I'd never used one.
Mom wore a big watch over hers. She never took it off.
"The people who love you most," she said, "never tell you what they spent."
But on Tuesday, I needed one.
At six p.m., Mom drove out to get my birthday cake.
She never came home.
So I rewound.
Seven a.m. Tuesday. I hid her car keys.
She took the bus. She never came home.
Rewind. I faked being sick.
She went at lunch instead. Rewind.
I begged. I cried. I locked every door.
Rewind. Rewind. Rewind.
Every Tuesday, at six, the phone rang.
I checked my wrist. A hundred. It never went down.
I thought I was the luckiest kid in town.
Sixty Tuesdays later, I stared at the empty cake box.
She only ever left for one thing.
So I made the cake myself.
Flour on the ceiling. Eggshells in the batter.
Six o'clock. She was still home.
Six thirty. She was laughing at my lopsided cake.
Seven. The phone never rang.
I hugged her so hard my arms hurt.
And for the first time in sixty days, I woke up on Wednesday.
