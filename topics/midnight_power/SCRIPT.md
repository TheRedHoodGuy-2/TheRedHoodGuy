# Midnight Power v1 (2026-11-26)
Title: Your superpower = whatever you're doing at midnight ⚡
COLD OPEN: POP, me in pyjamas and a cape appearing on the school roof at night, eyes wide, terrified. (= last shot)
1  In this town, you get one superpower on your twelfth birthday: whatever you're doing at exactly midnight.  [clock hits 12:00, a kid mid-bite glows]
2  My cousin was eating. Now he can eat a whole car.  [he chomps a car door, burps]
3  Dad was snoring. His snore knocks down fences.  [ZZZ, fence flattens]

7  Tuesday. My birthday. 11:55 p.m.  [clock on screen, tick]
8  I stood on my bed in my cape.
9  11:57. Mia turned on the vacuum. I didn't move.  [roar, cape flapping]
10 11:58. Mom yelled, "Bed! Now!" I didn't move.
11 11:59. The dog jumped on the bed. I wobbled. I didn't fall.  [bounce]
12 Ten seconds. I bent my knees.  [countdown on screen]
13 Three. Two.
14 One. I jumped.  [mid-air, arms out]
