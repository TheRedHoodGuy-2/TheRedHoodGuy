## Shared formula (all five)
1. Caption from frame 0, always visible, never animated. 2. Frame 0 is already mid-action, camera already moving. 3. One feeling per shot, faces carry the story (decal swaps on the beat). 4. Camera 4-100 %/s, no static camera except one deliberate hold at the punchline. 5. Cut ON the action; transition = flash, object, or eye-zoom, not a dissolve. 6. Ends mid-action or on an unresolved image; loops to the caption. 7. Visual spectacle (lens flare, sparkle, god rays, gas volume, muzzle flash, confetti) is added exactly where the emotion peaks.
## Differences
- Hawks = full 60 fps hand-keyed motion, long ~2 s shots, two-state gags, acting-heavy. Cody = 30 fps source (on twos), 7-12 s, face-and-VFX-heavy, caption jokes with a punch image.
- Neither relies on texture resolution; lighting and VFX carry the HQ feel.
