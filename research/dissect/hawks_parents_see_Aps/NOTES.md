## Lessons
- Cuts: 16 auto shots but only ~7 real cuts in 15.8 s; 5 flash/whip frames count as ONE transition (8 frames through white). Shots 0.7-4 s.
- The hook IS motion + an unresolved threat (hand reaching for a spider) in the first 0.5 s, plus the dual-caption promise.
- Reality shots: grounded, small believable acting (finger pain, hands on head). Fantasy shots: big wide with sun flare, motion blur, camera speeds of 60-100 %/s. Contrast of scale/speed between the two states IS the joke.
- Transition between states: object-based flash (flashlight beam / white-out), a swing, or a hammer rolling to the lens. Never a plain dissolve.
- Ends on an unresolved action (hammer wind-up), no punchline hold. Caption always visible, never animated.
- Graphics: Roblox avatars, many in imported catalog costumes (Spider-Man, Iron Man, Thor) with metal/cloth textures; lighting = sun + bounce + lens flare; rooms are flat-painted wood floors. Faces: dot-eye decals, big laughing mouths.
- For our channel: caption-pair structure and the reality-vs-fantasy scale contrast are reusable formula; lighting/flare/motion blur is the quality lever.
