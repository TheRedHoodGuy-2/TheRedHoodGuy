## Hard facts (all 9)
- Line 1 = "In this world/town ..." rule, voice starts at 0.00 s in EVERY video; "You see," lands in sentence 2.
- One-word captions, white bold with black outline, lower-middle of the frame, every spoken word.
- Gneef/GreenBro: 5.6-9 cuts per 10 s (a cut every 1.1-1.8 s), 243-284 wpm. SensBloxx: 2.4-3.2 cuts per 10 s, 187-210 wpm, so slower and cinematic.
- Every ending is a PAYOFF with the rule turned into something warm or absurd (kid gets a dinner with mom, town walks upside down, moon goes dark, whole town sleeps on one tug). Not one is a cliffhanger. NOTE: this contradicts our CLAUDE.md "most scripts BUILD to an unresolved cliffhanger". In this sample 9/9 resolve. Flag to Xavier.
- Last lines are a short "now / every night I ..." image of the new normal, 1-2 sentences, loop-friendly.
- Narrator is first person kid; consistent structure: rule -> 3-4 life gags -> "but I..." personal flaw/plant -> crisis -> clever use of the rule -> new normal.
## Frame 0 and the hook (viewed)
- Gneef Never Awake: frame 0 is a tilted wide night street, a pale doll-like creature already in frame, cut to its face in a window at 1.8 s (low angle, blue-purple moonlight), cut at 4.4 s to a long street with it small in the middle. Light = a cold blue key with strong colour grade; mood from light, not detail.
- Gneef String On Back: frame 0 the kid already walking away from camera, strings going up into a blue sky; at 2.3 s the camera swoops up along the strings into the clouds (fast tilt); 4.0 s cut to two characters holding hands facing the lens. Camera does the "wow" move on the rule's key image in second 2.
- GreenBro Hug: frame 0 two characters already walking toward each other on a suburban street; the hug demonstration plays out in the first 6 s with a white puff "poof" and age swap shown literally at 5.1-5.7 s. So rule is both SAID and SHOWN by 6 s.
- SensBloxx Water/Fire: frame 0 a crouched character on mud with a cinematic fly-through; 0.6-1.0 s whip zoom out to a whole river map with fire/water arcs. Higher polygon, volumetric clouds, real lighting, UE-looking. Gneef is the Roblox-engine look but high quality.
## What this means for our videos
1. Our target of Gneef-pace (6 cuts/10 s, 260 wpm) is confirmed; SensBloxx is the slow lane, not for us.
2. Show the rule in the first 6 s (hug-puff / counter ticking / strings up).
3. Big visible counter or strong single prop that drives the clock.
4. Ending = payoff + new normal image. Re-check our cliffhanger rule against this: ask Xavier.
5. Light colour per mood (cold blue horror vs warm sunny vs cinematic).
