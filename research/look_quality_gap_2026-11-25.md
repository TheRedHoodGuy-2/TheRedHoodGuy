# Why the reference videos look "high quality" and ours look low (2026-11-25, Xavier's ynbloxtv ref + Gneef)
- The ref is only 576x1024. Resolution is NOT the gap; full-res stills of ours are sharp. Turning off DOF / grain / CA /
  bloom and lifting the grade (tested on Sunset Tag t=45.6 / 24.6) barely changed anything.
- The gap is the LOOK: the ref uses clean flat colours, crisp simple textures, soft bright even light with gentle AO,
  little shadow contrast, and backgrounds in focus. Ours mixes photo-real busy textures (asphalt, leaf cards), low-res
  catalog clothing prints stretched over the body (the blurry "KS" shirt), a dark contrasty grade, heavy DOF.
- Plan (next video): a "clean" look preset = flat crisp materials (no photo textures on big surfaces), high-res or
  flat-colour clothing, bright soft key + strong sky fill, DOF off or light, no grain / CA, PNG intermediates. Test on
  one still vs the ref before any render.
