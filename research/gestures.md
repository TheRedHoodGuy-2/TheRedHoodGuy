# Gestures (2026-11-19)
## "Cut it" / "stop talking" (throat slash) for "We never talk about Aunt Linda"
- Western reading: a finger drawn across the throat = "cancelled / cut it / shut up"; a FULL FLAT HAND swept
  horizontally across the throat = the benign professional "cut" signal (TV / radio to the director, divers: out of air).
  An open palm-down hand near the neck twisting side to side several times = "stop speaking" (time is up).
  (TV Tropes Throat-Slitting Gesture; codex-mundi throat-slice entry; usingenglish.com gesture guide; symbols.com.)
- Our R6 version: FOREARM action (shoulder becomes the elbow, limbTo with an elbow point by the ribs): the hand block
  flat, palm down, at throat height, swept from one side of the neck to the other twice, quick, while he looks straight
  into the lens with a flat "don't ask" face (eyes half-lidded, mouth shut). Other arm relaxed. Head tilts a touch.
