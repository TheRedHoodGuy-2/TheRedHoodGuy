# House design research (2026-11-18) — base for the two house design boards

## Real floor plans (US two-storey family homes, about 2,500 sq ft, 4 bedrooms)
- Typical room sizes from a real 2,500 sq ft 4-bed plan: family room 17'6" x 19'9", living room 12' x 14'4",
  kitchen 13'6" x 12'6", dining 13' x 13', breakfast nook 10'8" x 8', master bedroom 18'2" x 13'3", other bedrooms
  about 11' x 11', 2-car garage 20' x 20'. Source: https://www.builderhouseplans.com/plan/print/2500-square-feet-4-bedroom-2-50-bathroom-2-garage-sp191691
- Modern two-storey plans put the open social rooms downstairs and all bedrooms upstairs (living + kitchen + dining
  open plan on the main floor). Sources: https://www.architecturaldesigns.com/house-plans/2-story-modern-home-plan-with-all-bedrooms-upstairs-85310ms ,
  https://archivaldesigns.com/products/contemporary-house-2124-square-feet-4-bedrooms-2-5-bathrooms-riverglade-24638
- Ceilings 9' on both floors in modern plans (https://www.architecturaldesigns.com/house-plans/modern-2-story-house-plan-under-3700-square-feet-with-4-upstairs-bedrooms-23447jd).
- OUR SCALE: Roblox doors are 1.65x a person (vs 1.16x real), so real room sizes x ~1.4 = our rooms: living ~5 x 6 m,
  kitchen ~4.8 x 4.5 m, bedrooms ~4.3 x 4.3 m, ceilings 4.1 m (scale v2).

## What "modern" looks like in 2026 (so the houses read current, not dated)
- Cold grey and stark white are out; warm neutrals are in (creamy white, sand, caramel, warm taupe, terracotta, ochre).
  Sources: https://www.yankodesign.com/2026/05/24/the-7-interior-design-trends-actually-defining-2026-homes/amp/ ,
  https://www.redfin.com/blog/interior-design-trends-2026/
- Warm minimalism: simple, intentional rooms with earthy tones, layered textures, natural materials.
  Source: https://www.homesandgardens.com/interior-design/warm-minimalism-decor-trend
- Kitchens: away from all-white; white oak and deeper walnut wood, painted cabinets in deep colours (teal, dark blue,
  taupe), brass fixtures, an island as the social hub. Sources: https://www.sierralivingconcepts.com/blog/interior-design-trends-according-to-experts/amp/ ,
  https://www.apartmenttherapy.com/home-trends-still-in-for-2026-37686257
- Bathrooms: warm vanity colours (terracotta, sandy beige, taupe, sage), floating vanities, clear glass shower panels,
  statement tile. Sources: https://www.modernbathroom.com/blog/post/2026/03/23/2026-bathroom-ideas-and-trends-you-ll-want-to-copy ,
  https://www.blockrenovation.com/guides/design/10-modern-bathroom-designs-that-feel-very-2026
- Teen boy room: study + gaming + sleep zones; deep blue, charcoal, khaki green, warm neutrals; warm walls and real
  wood with ambient LED light, cable-managed desk. Sources: https://houseofisabella.co.uk/pages/bedroom-ideas-for-teenage-guys ,
  https://www.osmoz.com/inspiration/osmoz-magazine/1774/13-cozy-gamer-bedrooms-that-actually-feel-like-a-vibe ,
  https://stealthygaming.com/modern-teen-boy-gaming-bedroom-ideas/
- Teen girl room: soft pinks, cool blues, personal wall (neon sign / art), plants, flexible zones (study, sleep,
  hang out). Sources: https://lamomoneon.com/blogs/news/10-creative-girls-room-ideas-to-transform-your-space-in-2026 ,
  https://palmera.com.tr/en/blogs/news/genc-odasi-dekorasyon-trendleri-2026-nin-en-yeni-trendleri

## The two houses (decisions in the boards: docs/sheets/house_A_board.jpg, house_B_board.jpg)
- House A, "warm modern" (the guy's family): white-oak floors, creamy walls, walnut and black accents, olive and
  terracotta soft goods, a dark-teal kitchen. His room: charcoal + deep blue + warm wood, LED strip at the desk.
- House B, "soft organic" (the family where the daughter is the child): lighter: pale oak, sage-green kitchen with
  brass, clay and cream, terracotta bathroom tile. Her room: blush + lavender, plants, fairy lights, neon sign.
- Both: open living / kitchen / dining downstairs + a powder room (toilet) + laundry/storage; all bedrooms upstairs +
  a family bathroom (shower) + the parents' en-suite; finished basement (A) vs unfinished storage basement (B); attic
  storage under the roof.
