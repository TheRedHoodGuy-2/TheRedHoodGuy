# Look study: our old videos vs Gneef (2026-11-18)

Sources: 6 of our Roblox Shorts (@og.theredhoodguy) and 6 Gneef winners, 24 frames each (240 frames total), sheet out/look_study.jpg.

| Measure (median) | Ours | Gneef |
|---|---|---|
| Brightness | 0.49 | 0.31 |
| Saturation | 0.38 | 0.57 |
| Darkest 3% | 0.04 (milky) | 0.00 (true black) |
| Brightest 3% | 0.88 | 0.76 |
| Warm tint (red minus blue) | 0.00 | 0.04 |
| Fine detail / noise | 0.064 | 0.043 |

Why ours looks flat and cheap:
1. Light comes from everywhere at once (big even fill): no shadow side on faces or props, nothing feels lit by a window or a lamp.
2. Exposure too high and blacks lifted: the image is milky, never deep.
3. Colour is pale and cold (light-blue walls, grey floor, white uniforms): one cold hue fills the frame.
4. Everything is equally sharp and busy: the background competes with the subject; the "detail" number is high partly from render noise.
5. Walls and floors are flat paint; very little surface pattern.

What Gneef does instead:
1. One motivated light direction per scene (window, lamp, moon, sun) and real shadows; rim light separates characters.
2. Darker overall with true blacks, highlights kept off white.
3. Rich, warm colour indoors vs cool outside; each place has its own colour.
4. Background darker and softer than the subject.
5. Pattern and texture on every surface: wood grain, knit bedding, striped wallpaper, patterned rugs.

Sourcing models: with the throwaway account's cookie, the Roblox Creator Store (free models: furniture, houses, props, built at
Roblox scale so they fit R6 without guessing sizes) is searchable and downloadable. Converting multi-part models is the next tool.
