# Sound effects bank: where the sounds come from (2026-11-19)
Rule (Xavier): sound effects ONLY from a real sound bank, never synthesized. Monetised YouTube needs commercial-safe,
no-attribution licences. Safe sources:
- Pixabay sound effects: Pixabay Content License, commercial use, no attribution, 100k+ files. (vidiq / sfxengine / toolradar guides 2026)
- Mixkit: free for commercial projects incl. monetised YouTube, no attribution, no sign-up.
- Freesound: 700k+ sounds but licence is PER SOUND (CC0 / CC-BY / CC-BY-NC): CC0 only, filter by licence.
- Kenney: CC0 game sound packs (UI, impacts, footsteps).
- Sonniss GDC bundles: 200+ GB pro SFX, royalty-free commercial, no attribution (great for foley: doors, footsteps, cloth).
- NOT allowed: BBC Sound Effects (no commercial use), Freesound CC-BY-NC.
Bank layout (to build): assets/sfx/<category>/<name>.wav + catalog.json {file, source, licence, url, tags, duration}:
footsteps (wood, tile, carpet, concrete, grass, stairs), doors (open, close, slam, knock, creak, garage), house (fridge,
microwave, faucet, toilet flush, shower, light switch, TV on), body (sit, stand, cloth rustle, slap, punch, fall),
objects (phone buzz, notification, cup, plate, keys, bag drop), outside (car pass, car door, bird, dog bark, wind),
Roblox-culture (oof-style: NOT Roblox's own sounds, they're copyrighted), whooshes and comedic stings.
Downloading: the cloud can't reach those sites; Pixabay / Mixkit downloads go through the laptop bridge or Xavier sends files.
