In this town, everyone is tied to their own front door. You see, on the day you're born, a rope grows from your belt to your doorknob, and it never comes off. Kids get twenty steps of rope. Grown-ups get a hundred. And if you go past the end, it snaps you home like a yo-yo. So our whole town is one big knot. The mailman has to jump rope down every street. Dog walkers get tied up twice a day. And my mom and our neighbor have been stuck in the same tangle since Tuesday. But my rope is the shortest in town. Our house is at the top of the hill, so I can't even reach the playground. Every day, I stand at the end of my rope and watch the other kids on the swings. So I stay home and fix things. Wobbly chairs. Leaky sinks. And our squeaky front door. I've taken it off its hinges so many times, I can do it with a spoon.
