Title: Your Midnight Move Becomes Your Power ⚡
Cold open: night, school roof, me in pyjamas + cape, eyes forced wide, moth drifting at my face.
1. In this town, whatever you do at midnight becomes your superpower. [clock flips 12:00]

3. Dad was snoring. Now his snore flattens fences.
4. The mailman was running late. Now he's the fastest man alive. Still late.
5. But I'd planned mine for a year. [crayon flight plan on the wall]
6. At midnight, I'd be jumping off my bed. I'd fly.
7. I told my whole class. [kid flaps his arms at me]
8. My little sister Mia wanted proof. All week she followed me with Dad's old camera. [FLASH, FLASH]
9. Birthday night. 11:55. [clock on screen]
10. I stood on my bed in my cape. Mia stood in the doorway, camera up.
11. 11:57. The dog jumped up. I wobbled. I didn't fall.
12. 11:58. Mom yelled, "Get in bed!" I was on the bed. Technically.
13. 11:59. Dad snored down the hall. My window blew open. Wind hit my face. I didn't blink.
14. Ten seconds. I bent my knees.
15. Three. Two. [Mia's thumb on the button]
16. One. I jumped.
